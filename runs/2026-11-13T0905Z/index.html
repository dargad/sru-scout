  • The upstream history is two commits: the Zed deprecation (b1d0feeb) and the Takashi change that restores the option (c197bbdc). The SRU needs to make clear which patches are included. The deprecation commit's release note may be inconsistent with the restored behavior, and the Launchpad bug is only referenced as 'Related-Bug' in the second commit.
